# AEEM Platform

The public website of the Africa Education Empowerment Movement (AEEM), where visitors learn about its work and get in touch with the team.

## Language

**Inquiry**:
A message a visitor sends to AEEM through the website, of exactly one Inquiry Type, that the team is expected to answer.
_Avoid_: Request, submission, contact request

**Inquiry Type**:
The kind of Inquiry: contact (a general question), volunteer (offering time), partner (an organization proposing to work with AEEM), or donor (offering money). "Contact" is one type, not another word for Inquiry.
_Avoid_: Category, form type
